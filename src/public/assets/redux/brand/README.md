# Built-in brand artwork

Application and publisher marks served from `assets/redux/brand/`. The paired
files carry the same drawing in two inks so a surface can always use the
higher-contrast version:

- `redux-app-icon.png` is the square product mark.
- `redux-wordmark-on-dark.png` and `redux-wordmark-on-light.png` are the
  product wordmark for dark and light surfaces.
- `redux-lockup-on-dark.png` and `redux-lockup-on-light.png` combine the product
  mark and wordmark for dark and light surfaces.
- `solst-ii-on-dark.png` and `solst-ii-on-light.png` are the publisher mark for
  dark and light surfaces.

The `on-dark` artwork uses near-white ink; the `on-light` artwork uses near-black
ink. Keep both variants together when replacing a mark.
